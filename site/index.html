    How to read the graph

    Leading

    Stronger than the benchmark and still gaining. RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum both above 100.

    Weakening

    Still stronger than the benchmark, but relative momentum is fading. Often the next stop after Leading.

    Lagging

    Weaker than the benchmark and still losing ground. Both readings below 100.

    Improving

    Still weaker than the benchmark, but relative momentum has turned up. Watch for a move into Leading.

    Rotation and heading

    Tails normally rotate clockwise. Heading is the direction of the last step: 0–90° (up and to the right) is the strongest direction, 180–270° the weakest. Long tails mean strong moves.

    Prices and updates

    Daily closes from Yahoo Finance, refreshed about every 30 minutes during US trading hours (9:30–16:00 New York) and again after the close. Weekly bars run Monday to Friday; the current week moves as it trades. To add a ticker that isn’t listed, add it to tickers.txt in the app’s GitHub project.

    The calculation

    RS = price ÷ benchmark. RS-Ratio = 100 × SMA(RS, 10) ÷ SMA(RS, 30) and RS-Momentum = 100 × RS-Ratio ÷ SMA(RS-Ratio, 9). Given the same prices, this reproduces StockCharts’ RRG values to the second decimal. Prices here are not adjusted for dividends, so high-yield ETFs such as XLE or XLU can read a few tenths away from StockCharts.
